"As Mg2+ and SO42− ions are respectively the second most abundant cation and anion present in seawater after Na+ and Cl−, magnesium sulfates are common minerals in geological environments. Their occurrence is mostly connected with supergene processes. Some of them are also important constituents of evaporitic potassium-magnesium (K-Mg) salts deposits."
